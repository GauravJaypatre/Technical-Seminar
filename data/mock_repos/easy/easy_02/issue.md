# Issue: Leap year function incorrectly identifies century years

**Task ID:** `easy_02`  
**Tier:** `easy`  
**Domain:** `Datetime / Calendar`  

## Description
`is_leap_year(year)` returns True for century years like 1900 and 2100 that are divisible by 100 but not divisible by 400. In the Gregorian calendar, century years are only leap years if divisible by 400.
